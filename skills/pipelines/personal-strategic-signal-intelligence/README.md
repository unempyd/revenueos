# Personal Strategic Signal Intelligence

**Turn your private information trail into source-grounded decision intelligence.** This skill helps executives, founders, and operators learn from read-later libraries, social bookmarks, highlights, notes, decisions, and applied work without confusing attention with conviction.

## What It Does

1. **Separates attention from conviction** — Saves are weak signals; reads, highlights, notes, decisions, applications, and measured outcomes carry progressively more weight.
2. **Prioritizes decisions before content** — Every promoted insight must inform a decision, experiment, build, offer, or monitored question before it becomes a content idea.
3. **Preserves source lineage** — Claims link to primary source records, and generated summaries cannot recursively count as new evidence.
4. **Protects private beliefs** — Inferred beliefs stay private, provisional, confidence-labeled, and separate from the user's stated views.
5. **Applies a quality gate** — A configurable expert panel reviews evidence, decision value, counterarguments, privacy, and measurability. The default pass threshold is 90/100.
6. **Runs on a sane cadence** — Weekly synthesis plus meaningful event triggers, not arbitrary six-hour summaries.

## Seven Capability Modules

| Module | Outcome |
|---|---|
| Attention Drift | Shows which themes are emerging, accelerating, fading, or moving toward application |
| Pre-Decision Oracle | Frames decisions the operator may be approaching as evidence-backed questions |
| Contradiction / Decision Court | Steelmans competing interpretations and identifies the test that could resolve them |
| Bookmark-to-Build | Converts recurring signals into a bounded prototype or operating experiment |
| Founder-IP Recombination | Recombines proven personal frameworks into distinct, attributable IP |
| Service-Offer Arbitrage | Finds offer hypotheses at the intersection of recurring pain and credible delivery advantage |
| Content Negative-Space | Maps strategically important ideas studied or applied privately but not yet addressed publicly |

## Signal Sources

The workflow can use any user-authorized combination of:

- read-later and browser-save libraries
- social bookmarks
- completed reads
- highlights and annotations
- personal notes and memos
- decision records
- experiments, prototypes, and shipped applications
- user-authorized public writing

Connectors are optional. Missing sources are reported rather than silently replaced with stale summaries.

## Usage

Copy this directory into the skills location used by your AI coding agent, then try:

- "Run my weekly strategic signal review."
- "Show attention drift, but keep attention separate from conviction."
- "Use Decision Court on my conflicting notes about this investment."
- "Which recurring bookmarks are strong enough to become a build?"
- "Find content negative-space, but do not publish private inferred beliefs."
- "Map service-offer opportunities supported by applied evidence."

## Typical Output

A weekly brief includes:

- the strongest decision-relevant signal
- attention versus conviction by theme
- candidate decisions and reversible tests
- the most useful contradiction
- one bounded build or experiment
- one optional content opportunity
- private inferred beliefs in a clearly separated private section
- source lineage, connector gaps, and unresolved questions

## Privacy and Security

- Use read-only, least-privilege connector scopes.
- Keep credentials in protected secret storage, never in prompts or repositories.
- Minimize raw private text sent to models.
- Carry privacy labels from sources into derived outputs.
- Require explicit approval before publishing, sending, or modifying external systems.
- Support revocation, deletion, and re-sync.
- Fail closed when source authorization or lineage cannot be verified.

## Requirements

- An AI coding agent capable of reading `SKILL.md`
- At least one user-authorized signal source
- Optional connectors for read-later, social bookmarks, notes, or decision records

No source-specific connector or credential is bundled with this skill.
