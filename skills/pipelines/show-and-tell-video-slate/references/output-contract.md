# Output Contract

Return these sections:

1. **Decision summary:** discovered, combined, ready, repair, deferred, and rejected counts.
2. **Combination table:** final episode, absorbed ideas, shared mechanism, combination rationale, and deferred material.
3. **Ranked slate:** thesis, viewer, business value, artifact, claim status, proof, pickups, exact 15-minute plan, opening promise, episode scores, and readiness.
4. **Packaging:** exactly three rows per episode with lane, exact title, zero-to-four-word thumbnail copy, component inventory, hook logic, claim risk, dimension scores, weighted score, and verdict.
5. **Shoot order:** business value, proof, demoability, click potential, effort, and learning value.
6. **Short-form routes:** at least three complete clip atoms per selected episode.
7. **Evidence boundary:** every claim that cannot yet be made safely.

Recommend one winning package per episode and state whether it is ready or conditional on proof.
