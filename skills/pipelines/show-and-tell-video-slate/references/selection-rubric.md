# Selection Rubric

## Episode gate

Score each lens from 0-100:

| Lens | Question |
|---|---|
| Viral hook | Is the result or consequential verdict immediately compelling? |
| YouTube potential | Can it earn the click and sustain a 15-minute session? |
| Creator fit | Is the episode grounded in work the creator actually did? |
| Buyer value | Does the target viewer gain a useful business decision? |
| Differentiation | Does first-party access make the episode difficult to copy? |
| Short-form yield | Can it produce at least three complete, distinct clips? |
| Engagement | Is there an earned opinion or decision worth discussing? |
| Demoability | Can the outcome and mechanism be shown clearly? |
| Payoff integrity | Does the episode fully deliver the package and opening promise? |

Require a 90+ average. Demoability and Payoff Integrity must each score at least 90. These are production-priority scores, not performance forecasts.

## Proof ladder

Prefer:

1. native result readback plus repeatable workflow demonstration;
2. verified output or before/after plus mechanism walkthrough;
3. working build or agent run with a labeled test result;
4. prototype or planned workflow without an achieved outcome;
5. opinion or framework without an artifact.

Items 1-2 may be `ready`. Item 3 normally requires repair. Combine or reroute items 4-5.

## Packaging gate

| Code | Dimension | Weight |
|---|---|---:|
| F | Claim and content fidelity | 20% |
| I | Channel identity fit | 20% |
| H | Hook specificity and stakes | 20% |
| C | Title-thumbnail complementarity | 15% |
| M | Mobile legibility | 15% |
| E | Performance evidence and differentiation | 10% |

Require 9.0+ overall and every dimension at 8.5+. Also require:

- one verdict, one proof, and one utility package;
- zero to four thumbnail words;
- no more than three major visual groups plus one text block;
- one focal relationship understandable at feed size;
- first-party proof for achieved numerical claims;
- visible labels for estimates, tests, and planned outcomes.

If all three packages cannot pass honestly, repair the episode promise. Never inflate scores to satisfy the gate.
